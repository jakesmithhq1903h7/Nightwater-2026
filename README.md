## 🕹️ About

Nightwater is a single-player open-world automation, crafting, resource-management, and incremental simulation game. Stranded on a solitary island floating through a sea of liquid void and starlight, you gather resources, build factories, expand into new biomes, and advance from primitive tools to futuristic technology. Uncover the secrets of the Lighthouse and find a way to escape this mysterious reality. Developed by Studio Ashty and published with Irox Games, Nightwater is planned for release in Q3 2026.

Nightwater is for players who enjoy relaxing exploration, incremental progression, factory automation, crafting, and uncovering atmospheric mysteries at their own pace.

## ✨ Features

- 🗺️ **Expand the Island** — Discover new biomes, resources, secrets, and mysterious places beyond the starting island.
- ⛏️ **Gather and Progress** — Collect resources by hand and complete milestones to unlock recipes, buildings, and upgrades.
- 🤖 **Automate Everything** — Build farmers, assemblers, storage units, pipes, and production chains that work for you.
- ⚙️ **Advance Through the Ages** — Progress from sticks and stones through the Bronze Age, Industrial Era, and beyond.
- 🎮 **Optional Minigames** — Play five unique minigames with independent progression, upgrades, and rewards.
- 🔦 **Uncover Hidden Truths** — Piece together an optional personal story with secret areas and four possible endings.
